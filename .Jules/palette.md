## 2026-10-03 - Add Keyboard Shortcut Hints
**Learning:** Users often miss keyboard shortcuts hidden in documentation. Exposing them in native tooltips (titles) and accessible `aria-keyshortcuts` attributes increases discoverability and usability for power users and screen readers alike.
**Action:** Add a `shortcut` prop to reusable buttons to automatically generate comprehensive tooltips and `aria-keyshortcuts` attributes.
